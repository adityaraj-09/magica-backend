## Start here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the app and sign in. Your tasks, library, projects, and credits stay on this account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new task">
    Describe the result you want. You do not need to pick a tool first — the agent chooses when a tool is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the run">
    Watch thinking, streamed text, and tool cards. When the turn finishes, the row under the reply shows duration, tokens, credits, and the routed model.
  </Step>
</Steps>
